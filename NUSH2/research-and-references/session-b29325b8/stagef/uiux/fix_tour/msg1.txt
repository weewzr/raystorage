Match the process tour to the engine and fix its focus and keys

Every tour number now comes from the live reply. The results rows and
data/tour_rows.json are gone; the tube limit, the pipeline condition of
the compressed CO2 and the tail gas power are stated in words.

On an infeasible plant, steps 2 and 3 read the IHX duty and process heat
at the need, and step 12 gives the branch and turbine output at the need
with the generation at the rated output, so generation and import add up
to the demand. Step 12 splits the branch as the engine does. Step 13 says
when abatement above the target is not counted as met, and credits the
recycle for part of the CO2 never formed. Scenario titles, notes and lit
lines follow the reject route and Selexol. HTGR, TRISO, MEA and INL are
written out at first use, and a no-break space joins each number to its
unit.

Start brings the card head into view and focuses the step title, or a bar
control when the title would be out of view. Step changes keep the card
head in view. Enter or Space on a unit or stream, or a choice from the
unit list, pauses the tour. The card has its own Previous and Next and a
line naming the keys, and the lead unit's selection ring stays off while
the tour runs.

The self test checks steps 2, 3, 12 and 13 at 100 MWth and Case A, the
unit spacing and the focus after Start, and audits every scenario
branch. CI also runs the self test under reduced motion.
