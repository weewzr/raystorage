# 5. Discussion and Conclusion

## 5.1 Discussion

**Environmental performance.** The plant abates {{A_ATM_B_MT|u:MtCO2e/y}} at {{PER_PASS_CAPTURE_PCT|f0}}% per-pass and {{PLANT_CAPTURE_B_PCT}}% plant capture, and the result holds across the baseline bounds. It rests on working capture and on recycle transferring to MEA.

**Economic performance.** The full cost of {{AC_L5_BOAK_60Y|u}} to {{AC_L5_FOAK_25Y|u}} at $n = 1.0$ misses the target in every central case, by both methods. The MATURE_60Y scenario reaches {{STEPDOWN_4_L5|u}} only with a tenth-of-a-kind reactor over 60~[[y]] and gas at {{STEPDOWN_4_GAS_S_GJ|u}}.

**Comparison with conventional SMR plus CCS.** IEAGHG Case 3 at {{RES_COMPARATOR_CAC_TS60_S_T|u}} on matched T&S sits above the NUS-H₂ BOAK case and below its FOAK case, so the proposal competes only once a reactor is past first of a kind. The comparison is indicative. NUS-H₂ remains distinct by removing the furnace, with residual emissions of {{INTENSITY_B_KG_KG|u}}.

**Implementation in Singapore.** Reactor qualification, the nuclear-chemical heat interface, site-specific emergency planning and contracted CO₂ transport and storage form the main requirements ({fig:f_road}). A competing pathway exists: Air Liquide and Aster have signed a memorandum on autothermal reforming with integrated capture in Singapore [@aster2025].

**Principal uncertainties.** Reactor size treatment spans {{AC_L5_BOAK_25Y|u}} to {{AC_L5_BOAK_25Y_N0P31|u}} at BOAK, 25~[[y]]. The gas saving, the SRD on syngas, IHX performance and the spare on Ahn and Lee's heat basis all remain unresolved. The column models and the Level 3 flowsheet failed their gates, so most terms stay anchored (Appendix E).

## 5.2 Conclusion

NUS-H₂ pairs a {{R_RATED_MWTH|f0|u}} HTGR with an SMR and 30~[[wt%]] MEA at SOXAL scale. The abatement target is met at {{A_ATM_B_MT|u:MtCO2e/y}}. The cost target of {{TARGET_COST_S_T|f0|u}} is missed in every central case, with a base full cost of {{AC_L5_BOAK_25Y|u}} at BOAK, 25~[[y]]. It is met only under the stated MATURE_60Y conditions, a tenth-of-a-kind reactor over 60~[[y]] with gas at {{STEPDOWN_4_GAS_S_GJ|u}}. Singapore's next steps are reactor and IHX qualification overseas, site-specific safety and licensing, hydrogen offtake and a contracted CO₂ storage pathway.
