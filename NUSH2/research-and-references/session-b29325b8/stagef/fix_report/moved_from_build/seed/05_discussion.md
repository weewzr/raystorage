Comparison with conventional SMR + CCS. The IEAGHG reference [@r5] supplies an independent SMR-CCS flowsheet, utility and cost methodology. Its shifted-syngas capture case uses MDEA and its MEA case treats reformer flue gas; the proposed design therefore adopts the IEAGHG methodology and takes MEA regeneration benchmarks from pilot studies [@r31,r32]. Singapore-specific costs and the common hydrogen-production basis will determine the final comparison.

Implementation in Singapore. Jurong Island offers established industrial infrastructure and potential hydrogen offtake. Reactor qualification, the nuclear-chemical heat interface, site-specific emergency planning, licensing and contracted CO₂ transport and storage form the main project-development requirements. Principal uncertainties. Reactor and IHX performance, MEA regeneration heat recovery, operating availability, natural gas costs and CO₂ transport arrangements govern the proposed system’s performance. Integrated energy calculations and Singapore-specific project assessment will refine the screening design.

5.2 Conclusion

The proposed 230 [[MWth]] HTGR-assisted SMR + MEA CCS facility offers a pathway for lower-emissions industrial hydrogen production in Singapore. INL process conditions establish the reference reforming duty and production scale, and a 30 wt% MEA system captures process CO₂. The selected reactor rating provides a preliminary heat-and-electricity allocation for engineering assessment. Singapore’s next steps include reactor and IHX qualification, site-specific safety and licensing, hydrogen offtake and a contracted CO₂ storage pathway.
