Conclusions
© Siemens Energy, 2026
18
Global warming and the resulting energy transition is one of the biggest challenges 
for the next decades.
There is no “one” energy transition – every region and country needs to work from 
its individual position. Turbomachinery plays a key-role in all of them.
There is no single solution for the energy transition. It needs a mix of measures:
•
Substantial grid upgrades and expansion, including grid stability
•
Apply energy efficiency, particularly to transform the industry
•
Decarbonize molecules and use existing infrastructure as a bridge
•
More resilient raw material and supply chains (geo-politics)
Academia, Industry and Government must collaborate closely to develop 
technologies and solutions to meet the requirements of the energy transition.
