Commit the legacy tables of main 8750b09 and a keyed compare for them

The seven tables that main 8750b09 wrote are stored under
engine/vv/data/legacy_results/ as regression data for the legacy carbon basis.
They are the output of the engine before the co-funding model and the
independent carbon basis, taken with git show from that commit.

scripts/compare_results.py gains three options. --subset compares by key
instead of by position, so every row of the snapshot must be present with equal
cells while rows that later work added are counted and not failed. --names
restricts the comparison to named files. --allow-cell FILE:KEY:COLUMN names one
cell that may differ, which the entry count of the registry needs. Without the
options the script behaves as before.
