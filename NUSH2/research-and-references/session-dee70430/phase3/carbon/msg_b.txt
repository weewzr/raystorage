Compute the CO2 abatement from the plant's own carbon balance

The default carbon basis is now the plant's own stream ledger. The Level 3
flowsheet is solved on the hydrogen target, with the cryogenic carbon recovery
and hydrogen return as flagged registry design inputs (decision D8), and the
captured CO2, the stack, the condensate CO2, the product methane and the gas
rate are read from its streams (equations N.1 to N.5). The unabated reference
is the IEAGHG 2017-02 Case 1 intensity times the annual hydrogen (decision D2);
INL, Cho and a plant-built floor are comparison rows. Net abatement subtracts
transport and storage emissions built from physical inputs (decision D7c), and
the cost per tonne divides by that net figure (decision D9). Closure carries the
streams the flowsheet does not model as unknown, so the whole-account verdict
stays undetermined while the verdict on the modelled streams is closed.

The anchored functions keep their code. Model::plant_balance returns the same
balance with the carbon lines replaced by the ledger when CARBON_BASIS is 1, and
the consumers that divide by abatement now use it. The legacy basis stays
selectable (CARBON_BASIS 0, nush2 --basis legacy, the carbon_basis control of the
wasm reply) and is bit-identical: a Rust test and both workflows compare its
export with the snapshot of main 8750b09.

Default headline (Case B, central IEAGHG reference), as the engine prints it:
captured 363,277 t/y, stack 30,778 t/y, condensate CO2 1,731 t/y, direct
abatement 569,545 t/y, transport and storage 11,778 t/y, net abatement 557,767 t/y
(8.334 kg/kg), reduction 94.6 percent, intensity 0.4858 kg/kg, gas 23.10 MMSCFD.
The legacy basis gives 359,777, 38,309, 578,052 t/y and 23.23 MMSCFD.

Tables of results/ that change, and why:
- Integrated_Energy_Balance.csv: unchanged, by test.
- Emissions_Ledger.csv: 252 rows become 456. 204 rows of a new independent_carbon
  view (annual quantities, transport and storage by source, direct and net
  abatement against each reference, reduction, intensity, closure, the N.9.2
  split). 216 existing rows change because the carbon lines come from the ledger.
- Final_Design_Basis.csv: 15 input rows for the new registry entries, 7 result
  rows, and 84 changed results (carbon, gas, abatement and the cost rows).
- Integrated_Mass_Balance.csv: 63 rows, the carbon lines of the fixed anchored
  runs and the flowsheet runs at the 1e-12 level through the registry inputs.
- Economic_Ledger.csv: abatement_t_y and everything per tonne in all 1,880 changed
  rows, and the gas, capture and transport tariff lines through the plant's
  captured CO2 and gas rate.
- Sensitivity_Results.csv: denominators in 641 rows, 214 rows of new sweeps over
  the cryogenic inputs, the condensate vent, the central reference and the
  shipping distance, and 44 rows whose solved test value moved.
- Subsidy_Scenarios.csv: all 352 rows, because the abatement and the capture
  capital come from the new basis.
- Validation_Results.csv: 18 rows added (the independent basis against the copied
  INL values, the cryogenic inputs, the reference gas, the closure and the
  transport and storage cross-checks), the registry entry count of
  PARAMS_COMPLETE, and no change of any existing pass or fail: 32 literature
  benchmarks stay failed.

Tests: m11_carbon.rs checks every N equation by id and the properties of the
basis, basis_flag.rs checks the command line flag, and the tests that compared
the exporter or the cost sweeps with the legacy balance now use the plant basis.
The co-funding tests keep their literal anchors on the legacy basis and read the
committed tables on the default basis.
