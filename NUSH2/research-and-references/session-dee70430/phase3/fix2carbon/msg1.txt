Select the baseline of a sensitivity point through CARBON_BASELINE

The BASELINE group of the sensitivity table shifted the abatement to the
alternative intensity and left the GAS line on the central reference gas, which
breaks rule 1 of the boundary table of N.7 and disagrees with the
CARBON_BASELINE group. On the plant ledger a point that changes the baseline
now selects it through CARBON_BASELINE, so the gas and the abatement follow it
together. The base_value column prints the central intensity of the basis
(8.995997 kg/kg) and not INL's 9.209770. The offset of the abatement stays for
the anchored carbon, where it keeps the values committed at 8750b09.

Tests: rule 1 of N.7 at every sensitivity point that changes the baseline (the
three BASELINE points and the three CARBON_BASELINE points), and the legacy
offsets.
