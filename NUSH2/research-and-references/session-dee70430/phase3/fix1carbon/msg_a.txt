Price the reference gas that the central intensity implies and label the CO2e terms

Two findings of the independent verifier on the carbon track.

1. The cost per tonne depended on a copied INL plant result. The L0 GAS
   line, the gas lines of the DCF, the saved upstream gas and the gas keys
   of the case summary priced the gas saved against INL's conventional-plant
   gas (NG_CONV_NOCC, 52.5 MMSCFD scaled), while the abatement reference is
   the central intensity. Model::reference_carbon and
   Model::reference_gas_mmscfd (N.7.3, N.9.2) now give the one reference gas
   of the independent basis, and the ledger, the DCF, the lifecycle credit,
   the summary and the decomposition read it (rule 1 of N.7). The legacy
   basis returns INL's gas with the same expression as before. INL's gas is
   a comparison input (CostQuery::ng_smr) with three reported validation rows
   (IND_GAS_LINE_UNDER_INL_GAS, IND_L5_UNDER_INL_GAS,
   IND_LAC_DCF_UNDER_INL_GAS).

2. A CO2e quantity sat among CO2 rows. EF_UPSTREAM is a CO2e total at a
   100-year potential of 30 (IEA 2026) and EF_NUCLEAR is a CO2e range with no
   stated horizon. Lifecycle fields are now up_credit_co2e and nuclear_co2e;
   every row of lifecycle_burdens and lifecycle_upstream on the plant ledger
   says CO2e, names the horizon and the source; EF_UPSTREAM_GWP100 is added
   to the registry as the label of EF_UPSTREAM. The CO2 and CH4 split of
   N.9.5 is not made: the sources read give only the aggregate and a split
   would need an invented share. The wasm label of the lifecycle KPI says
   CO2e total.

Tests: rule 1 of N.7 (gas line, decomposition, lifecycle and summary read one
gas difference), INL's gas as a comparison with an exact cost effect, the
implied gas reproduces INL's gas within 0.5 percent on INL's own intensity,
legacy bit identity of the gas, the exported lifecycle equals the closed form
of N.9.5 on the aggregate factor, and a CO2e guard over every lifecycle view
