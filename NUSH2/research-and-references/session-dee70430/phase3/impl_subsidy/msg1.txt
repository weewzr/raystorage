Add the government co-funding model to the plant engine

Implements equations S.1 to S.9c of docs/derivation/02_cost_and_subsidy.md on
the annual net abatement that the caller passes in, so the legacy basis and the
independent basis can both use it.

subsidy.rs holds the grant plan (eligible capital, grant, private capital,
interest on the gross and on the private balance, the part the grant saves, the
dated GRANT flows), the funded-balance recursion and its closed form for a
grant at close, the ledger form at close, the reported quantities of both
methods, the strict target flag and the break-even solve. The progressive
break-even is the closed form of S.7b. At close the ledger uses S.7c and the
cash flow uses the exact piecewise-linear route of S.7d. Every closed-form
share is checked against the cost the model returns at that share, and a
residual above the registry tolerance sends the solve to a bracketed bisection.
A cost already below the target returns a share of 0, and a cost at or above
the target at the largest share returns the cost at that share and no share.
A share outside 0 to 1, or not a number, is refused.

economics.rs gains the grant field of CostQuery and the net-of-grant block of
the Ledger (grant credit and interest effect, after L5). dcf.rs gains dcf_at,
which takes the abatement as an argument, adds one GRANT line and one GRANT gap
term when the share is not zero, and leaves the path unchanged otherwise. The
existing rx_mult lever is untouched.

The registry follows decision D7b for definition B. The derivation table of S.1
lists another membership (power island in, capture plant and recycle train
out); tests/m10_subsidy.rs reproduces the derivation numbers by setting those
flags.

tests/m10_subsidy.rs has 36 tests named after the equation they check. The
closed forms in it are written from the derivation, not through the engine.
Workspace tests: 409 passed. The seven tables regenerate byte for byte.
