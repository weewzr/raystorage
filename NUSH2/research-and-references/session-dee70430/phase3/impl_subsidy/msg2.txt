Write the co-funding scenarios as an eighth results table

The exporter gains Subsidy_Scenarios.csv for the headline run B_REC_F at n = 1.0
and the rated reactor: four stages, lives of 25 y and 60 y, eligible sets A and
B, both timings, the four shares of the registry grid for the ledger (L5) and
the discounted cash flow, and the break-even rows for each method and for both
together. Every row carries total, eligible, granted and private capital,
interest during construction on the gross spend, on the private balance and the
part the grant saves, the annual cost without and with the grant, the annual
abatement, the cost per tonne, the strict target flag, the public outlay per
tonne, the state-side present value and the gap between the two methods. A
break-even share is printed rounded up in the sixth decimal and moved up a digit
if the strict test still fails, so a printed share always passes.

The seven existing tables are byte-identical. Validation_Results.csv changes in
one cell: the conditions of EXPORT_BYTE_IDENTICAL read "Seven plant tables",
because the exporter now builds seven tables in the plant crate.

Every hard-coded list of the tables is updated: the CLI, compare_results.py,
build_site.py, the pointer test (eight files), the README and the results list
of the design record. build_numbers.py gains a subsidy dimension in the ledger
key (eligible set, timing, share in percent or BE for the break-even row) and
tokens for the new table, and checks that a share of zero reproduces the
Economic ledger.

Tests: three more in tests/m10_subsidy.rs. Two compare the committed table with
the engine and with the Economic ledger at a zero share. One checks, for all 64
combinations of stage, life, eligible set, timing and method, that the residual
at the break-even share is below 1e-9 and that the cost passes just above and
fails just below it. Workspace tests: 412 passed.
