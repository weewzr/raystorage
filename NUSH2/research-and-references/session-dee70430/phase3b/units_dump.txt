CAPTION Layout follows the project's Figure 2. The dashed line holds the reformer train, the MEA plant, the recycle compressor, the cryogenic unit, the CO₂ compressor and the ship to offshore storage. The PSA sits outside it as an external block with no simulation, and its reject crosses the line on the way back. Offshore storage is a tariff with no simulation. Stream tags are the stream ids of results/Integrated_Mass_Balance.csv, and a ring marks a stream that file lists as crossing the boundary; the feed, steam and condensate cross at their open ends. Lines tagged P, H, L, C or R have no row in that file, since the flowsheet treats them as inside a unit. The reformer block holds S04, the shift block S06 to S09A, the separator S09C, and the burner air COMBUSTION_O2 is not drawn. The drawing shows one helium circulator; the engine counts the secondary circulator too, in that panel. The recycle compressor and the cryogenic unit are drawn apart and modelled as one unit.
