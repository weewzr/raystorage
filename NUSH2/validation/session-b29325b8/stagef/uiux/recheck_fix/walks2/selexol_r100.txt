== 1 Fission heat in the reactor core
  [warn] Not feasible at these inputs. Infeasible: the process needs 165.9 MWth and the rating is 100.0 MWth, a deficit of 65.9 MWth. 18.2 MWth of heat duty sits above the rating, and grid import supplies electricity only.
  [scen] Scenario: PSA reject fate: INL Case 6 with Selexol. The drawing shows the headline plant, Case B with the reject recycled, so some drawn lines may carry nothing here.
  [text] Inside the core of the high-temperature gas-cooled reactor (HTGR), uranium nuclei in the tristructural isotropic (TRISO) fuel particles split when they absorb a neutron, and the graphite moderator slows the new neutrons so the chain reaction carries on.
  [text] The fission heat passes to helium flowing through the core, which leaves at 925 °C. The plant needs 165.9 MWth and the reactor is rated at 100 MWth.

== 4 Natural gas and steam feed the reformer
  [scen] Scenario: PSA reject fate: INL Case 6 with Selexol. The drawing shows the headline plant, Case B with the reject recycled, so some drawn lines may carry nothing here.
  [text] Natural gas at 23.2 MMSCFD and process steam enter the reformer, joined by the methane-rich recycle of step 9. In the catalyst tubes methane and steam react to hydrogen and carbon monoxide, taking up the helium's heat.

== 9 PSA reject back to the cryogenic unit
  [scen] Scenario: PSA reject fate: INL Case 6 with Selexol. The drawing shows the headline plant, Case B with the reject recycled, so some drawn lines may carry nothing here.
  [text] The PSA reject, still holding 1,226 kg/h of hydrogen, crosses back into the boundary and is compressed for the cryogenic unit.
  [text] The cold separation returns hydrogen to the PSA feed and a methane-rich recycle to the reformer. The nitrogen-rich cut is burned and leaves by the stack as the plant's residual 27,501 t/y of CO₂.

== 13 The plant from end to end
  [warn] Not feasible at these inputs. Infeasible: the process needs 165.9 MWth and the rating is 100.0 MWth, a deficit of 65.9 MWth. 18.2 MWth of heat duty sits above the rating, and grid import supplies electricity only.
  [scen] Scenario: PSA reject fate: INL Case 6 with Selexol. The drawing shows the headline plant, Case B with the reject recycled, so some drawn lines may carry nothing here.
  [text] End to end, the plant makes 66,925 t/y of purified hydrogen and sends 370,586 t/y of CO₂ to storage.
  [text] Against the 616,361 t/y an unabated reformer would emit on INL's baseline, its residual of 27,501 t/y leaves a net abatement of 588,860 t/y. Part of that is the CO₂ stored; the rest is never formed, because reactor heat replaces the reformer's fuel gas and the cryogenic recycle returns methane to the feed.
  [text] The net abatement is at or above the target of 250,000 t/y, but the engine does not count the target as met, since the plant is not feasible at these inputs. On Cho's and IEAGHG's baselines the net abatement is 598,245 t/y and 574,553 t/y.
