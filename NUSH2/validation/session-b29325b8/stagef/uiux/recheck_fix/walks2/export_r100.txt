== 1 Fission heat in the reactor core
  [warn] Not feasible at these inputs. Infeasible: the process needs 184.4 MWth and the rating is 100.0 MWth, a deficit of 84.4 MWth. 48.6 MWth of heat duty sits above the rating, and grid import supplies electricity only.
  [scen] Scenario: PSA reject fate: Export across the boundary. The drawing shows the headline plant, Case B with the reject recycled, so some drawn lines may carry nothing here.
  [text] Inside the core of the high-temperature gas-cooled reactor (HTGR), uranium nuclei in the tristructural isotropic (TRISO) fuel particles split when they absorb a neutron, and the graphite moderator slows the new neutrons so the chain reaction carries on.
  [text] The fission heat passes to helium flowing through the core, which leaves at 925 °C. The plant needs 184.4 MWth and the reactor is rated at 100 MWth.

== 13 The plant from end to end
  [warn] Not feasible at these inputs. Infeasible: the process needs 184.4 MWth and the rating is 100.0 MWth, a deficit of 84.4 MWth. 48.6 MWth of heat duty sits above the rating, and grid import supplies electricity only.
  [scen] Scenario: PSA reject fate: Export across the boundary. The drawing shows the headline plant, Case B with the reject recycled, so some drawn lines may carry nothing here.
  [text] End to end, the plant makes 66,925 t/y of purified hydrogen and sends 372,465 t/y of CO₂ to storage.
  [text] Against the 616,361 t/y an unabated reformer would emit on INL's baseline, its residual of 149,008 t/y leaves a net abatement of 467,353 t/y. Part of that is the CO₂ stored; the rest is never formed, because reactor heat replaces the reformer's fuel gas.
  [text] The net abatement is at or above the target of 250,000 t/y, but the engine does not count the target as met, since the plant is not feasible at these inputs. On Cho's and IEAGHG's baselines the net abatement is 476,738 t/y and 453,046 t/y.
