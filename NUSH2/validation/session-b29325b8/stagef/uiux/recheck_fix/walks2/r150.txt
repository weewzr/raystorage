== 1 Fission heat in the reactor core
  [warn] Not feasible at these inputs. Infeasible: the process needs 178.5 MWth and the rating is 150.0 MWth, a deficit of 28.5 MWth. The heat duties fit within the rating and the power branch falls short, so 12.65 MWe is imported.
  [text] Inside the core of the high-temperature gas-cooled reactor (HTGR), uranium nuclei in the tristructural isotropic (TRISO) fuel particles split when they absorb a neutron, and the graphite moderator slows the new neutrons so the chain reaction carries on.
  [text] The fission heat passes to helium flowing through the core, which leaves at 925 °C. The plant needs 178.5 MWth and the reactor is rated at 150 MWth.

== 4 Natural gas and steam feed the reformer
  [text] Natural gas at 23.2 MMSCFD and process steam enter the reformer, joined by the methane-rich recycle of step 9. In the catalyst tubes methane and steam react to hydrogen and carbon monoxide, taking up the helium's heat.
  [text] The engine sets the exit at 871 °C, the equilibrium assumption of the Idaho National Laboratory (INL) study. With helium supplied at 900 °C, the engine's tube model found no tube count in its search range that reaches it on the assumed tube, so the exit stays an assumption.

== 9 PSA reject back to the cryogenic unit
  [text] The PSA reject, still holding 1,226 kg/h of hydrogen, crosses back into the boundary and is compressed for the cryogenic unit.
  [text] The cold separation returns hydrogen to the PSA feed and a methane-rich recycle to the reformer. The nitrogen-rich cut is burned and leaves by the stack as the plant's residual 38,309 t/y of CO₂.

== 13 The plant from end to end
  [warn] Not feasible at these inputs. Infeasible: the process needs 178.5 MWth and the rating is 150.0 MWth, a deficit of 28.5 MWth. The heat duties fit within the rating and the power branch falls short, so 12.65 MWe is imported.
  [text] End to end, the plant makes 66,925 t/y of purified hydrogen and sends 359,777 t/y of CO₂ to storage.
  [text] Against the 616,361 t/y an unabated reformer would emit on INL's baseline, its residual of 38,309 t/y leaves a net abatement of 578,052 t/y. Part of that is the CO₂ stored; the rest is never formed, because reactor heat replaces the reformer's fuel gas and the cryogenic recycle returns methane to the feed.
  [text] The net abatement is at or above the target of 250,000 t/y, but the engine does not count the target as met, since the plant is not feasible at these inputs. On Cho's and IEAGHG's baselines the net abatement is 587,436 t/y and 563,745 t/y.
