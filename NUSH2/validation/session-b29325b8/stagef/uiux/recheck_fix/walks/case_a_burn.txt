== 1 Fission heat in the reactor core
  [scen] Scenario: Plant basis: Case A, INL reference; PSA reject fate: Burn on site, H2 held. The drawing shows the headline plant, Case B with the reject recycled, so some drawn lines may carry nothing here.
  [text] Inside the core of the high-temperature gas-cooled reactor (HTGR), uranium nuclei in the tristructural isotropic (TRISO) fuel particles split when they absorb a neutron, and the graphite moderator slows the new neutrons so the chain reaction carries on.
  [text] The fission heat passes to helium flowing through the core, which leaves at 925 °C. The reactor runs at 187.5 MWth of its 210 MWth rating.
  [figs] Reactor outlet925 °CFixed value

== 13 The plant from end to end
  [scen] Scenario: Plant basis: Case A, INL reference; PSA reject fate: Burn on site, H2 held. The drawing shows the headline plant, Case B with the reject recycled, so some drawn lines may carry nothing here.
  [text] End to end, the plant makes 97,946 t/y of purified hydrogen and sends 545,112 t/y of CO₂ to storage.
  [text] Against the 902,060 t/y an unabated reformer would emit on INL's baseline, its residual of 218,077 t/y leaves a net abatement of 683,984 t/y. Part of that is the CO₂ stored; the rest is never formed, because reactor heat replaces the reformer's fuel gas.
  [text] The abatement target of 250,000 t/y is met at these inputs. On Cho's and IEAGHG's baselines the net abatement is 697,719 t/y and 663,045 t/y.
